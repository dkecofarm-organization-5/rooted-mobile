# Volume Control v1.1.8

## Integrated command bar
Added:
- Mute / Unmute icon
- Volume down
- Current volume %
- Volume up

## Range
- 0% to 100%
- 5% increments
- default: 40%

## Behavior
The integrated player applies the selected volume to the currently active language runtime.
Switching KR / EN / RU keeps the same integrated volume setting.

The parent overrides the child runtime's hidden volume slider range to 0–100 while integrated.
